First list the canonical facts the repository supports: what the product does, for whom, what is built, any real usage numbers, pricing, team, plans. Slides: problem, solution, why now, market (sized only from given numbers), traction, product, team, ask. One idea per slide, short headlines, no filler. Cross-check every number against the list before finishing.
